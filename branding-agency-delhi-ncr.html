CHOOSING A
BRANDING AGENCY

A practical guide for businesses in Delhi NCR & Sonipat — updated August 2026

To choose a branding agency in Delhi NCR or Sonipat, evaluate three things: real portfolio work (not templates), clear pricing with defined deliverables, and how fast they respond during the enquiry stage. An agency that is slow before you pay will be slower after. Below is what to check, what branding actually costs in India, and the red flags to avoid.

  • Professional brand identity in India costs roughly ₹50,000–₹5,00,000+ depending on scope (2026)
  • A focused identity project takes 2–4 weeks; full brand + packaging takes 6–10 weeks
  • The single best predictor of quality is past work for businesses like yours

What does a branding agency actually do?

A branding agency defines how your business looks, sounds and feels everywhere it appears — logo, colours, typography, packaging, social media creatives and advertising. Good agencies start with strategy (who are your customers, what should they remember about you) before touching design. For example, when HAEROX built the identity for LUCID, a focus-drink brand, the work covered naming direction, logo system, can packaging direction and launch ad creatives — not just a logo file.

How much does branding cost in India in 2026?

Pricing varies widely by scope and agency size:

  • ₹10,000–₹30,000 — logo + basic identity from an independent designer
  • ₹50,000–₹1,50,000 — full identity (logo system, palette, typography, guidelines) from a boutique agency
  • ₹1,50,000–₹5,00,000+ — strategy-led branding with packaging, brand deck and launch campaign

Beware of quotes that look too cheap — they usually mean template logos, stock elements, or endless upsells later. Always ask for the exact deliverables list in writing.

What should you check before signing?

  • Portfolio depth: ask for 2–3 complete projects, not just logo thumbnails. A real brand project shows strategy, variations and application on packaging or ads.
  • Industry fit: an agency that has worked in your category (automotive, FMCG, hospitality) already knows what converts.
  • Deliverables in writing: file formats, number of revisions, timelines and ownership rights.
  • Response speed: if proposals take a week during sales, expect slower delivery after payment.
  • Who does the work: ask if work is done in-house or outsourced.

Red flags to avoid

  • No written proposal or contract — only WhatsApp promises
  • "Unlimited revisions" — sounds good, usually means no design direction
  • Portfolio full of concept work but no real, launched clients
  • Full payment demanded upfront with no milestone structure
  • No questions asked about your business before quoting a price

Local vs remote: does location matter?

For pure branding and digital work, portfolio quality beats proximity. Location matters when you need product photoshoots, on-ground campaign execution, or frequent in-person working sessions — in that case a Delhi NCR or Sonipat-based agency saves real time and travel cost. Common questions

How much does branding cost in India in 2026?

A basic logo package starts around ₹10,000–₹30,000 from independent designers. A full professional brand identity — strategy, logo system, colours, typography, guidelines — typically runs ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000+ depending on scope.

How long does a brand identity project take?

A focused identity project takes 2–4 weeks with a responsive agency. Larger projects with packaging, brand guidelines and launch campaigns take 6–10 weeks.

Should I hire a local agency or a remote one?

Portfolio quality matters more than location for most branding work. Go local (Delhi NCR/Sonipat) when you need shoots, on-ground campaigns or frequent in-person sessions.

What files should a branding agency deliver?

Minimum: vector logo files (AI/SVG/EPS), PNG exports, colour codes, typography choices and usage guidelines. Better agencies add social templates, packaging direction and a shareable brand deck.
